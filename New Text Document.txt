<h2>How We Met</h2>
<p>It all started with a <strong>matrimonial ID</strong>. One day, my mom showed me a matrimonial profile that caught my attention. At that time, I was the one handling the matrimonial profile, so I called her mom and introduced myself. I shared everything about me, my work, my family, and where I was in life. I was working in Coimbatore then, and during our conversation, she asked if my parents and I could meet her sometime while she was there in Coimbatore. I agreed, and that was the <strong>first time we met her mom and chithi</strong>. For me, it was a completely new experience. I had never met a girl's parents before for a marriage proposal, and there was something different about sitting in front of someone who was trying to understand whether I could become a part of their daughter's life. That conversation meant something, and she accepted me. After that first meeting, there were several days of conversations and discussions with relatives and friends. Slowly, the proposal started moving forward. Then one day, we got the call. They wanted to visit our house in Tirunelveli. That day finally came, with relatives, family members, expectations, and excitement all around us. By <strong>God's grace</strong>, everything went beautifully, and on <strong>June 14, our flower ceremony happened</strong>. What started with a matrimonial profile had already begun turning into something much bigger, something I couldn't have planned myself, something <strong>God was quietly writing</strong>.</p>

<h2>How God Planned Everything</h2>
<p>I have never seen a celebration like the one our families gave us. I was always someone who never really celebrated my birthday properly. There was never much excitement, no big birthday celebrations and no special moments that made me feel like life was something to celebrate. Many times, I would <strong>stand alone on the terrace</strong>, look at the moon and ask God, <strong>“When will I have the life I dream about?”</strong> I had so many dreams in my heart about love, family, happiness and having someone to share my life with. Even my friends sometimes told me that I was dreaming too much and that the life I imagined was not even possible. But I kept believing that <strong>one day God would answer that prayer</strong>. And then Melly came into my life. During the engagement preparations, I slowly realized that <strong>God had given me much more than what I had asked for</strong>. I was not just getting a wife, I was getting a <strong>beautiful family and a whole new life</strong>. Mummy, Sherle akka, Madhu Chithi, Sharon and Chitapa all accepted me and treated me like one of their own. There is one moment I will never forget. <strong>Mummy was the first person to hug me like that</strong>. In that moment, I felt something I had never felt before. She truly saw me as her own son. That hug touched me so deeply that <strong>I cried inside with joy and happiness</strong>. It was such a simple moment, but for me, it meant everything. Chithi would call me every day to check on me and send me lovely messages. Mummy prepared the royal lunch for me and my family. Sherle akka and Sharon gave me a Raksha Bandan gift and showed me so much love and support. These may look like small things to others, but for someone who spent so many days wishing for a life filled with love and family, these moments meant everything to me. Then came <strong>September 11, our engagement day</strong>. It was one of the happiest days of my life, and honestly, it felt like one of those dreams I had once asked God for while standing alone under the moon. I always had a special wish for <strong>11:11</strong>, so I asked Mummy if we could fix our marriage date on <strong>November 11</strong>. I never thought that this small wish would actually come true. But by God's grace, November 11 became our marriage date. When I look back now, I feel that <strong>God was listening to every prayer</strong>, even the ones I thought nobody heard. The life my friends thought was impossible became my reality. The girl I saw in a matrimonial profile became the girl I fell in love with, and the life I once only dreamed about became the life I am living today. God did not just give me Melly. He gave me the <strong>love, family, happiness and memories</strong> that I had been praying for all those years. This is more than the story of how I met a girl. This is the story of <strong>how God answered a boy</strong> who once stood alone on a terrace, looked at the moon and asked for the life he dreamed of.</p>
